About Us

More Than Desserts.

Luna’s began with a feeling.

The warmth of home.
The comfort of shared desserts.
The kind of happiness that lingers long after the final spoonful.

What started as a taste of the Philippines slowly became a ritual of comfort in Pune.

“Every dessert carries a memory. Ours just happens to taste like home.”

Meet Luna

THE HEART BEHIND LUNA’S

Luna is more than our inspiration. She’s the soul of everything we create.

Full of warmth, joy, and unconditional love, she reminds us that the best moments in life are often the simplest ones — shared desserts, laughter, and time spent together.

Every spoonful we serve carries that same feeling: soft happiness made to be shared.

Meet Yna

OUR FILIPINO CHEF

When Yna moved to Pune, she missed more than just home. She missed the feeling that came with it — comfort, warmth, and the way Filipino desserts bring people together.

So she began sharing a small piece of that world through the Mango Float — a dessert deeply rooted in Filipino celebrations, nostalgia, and togetherness.

Luna’s became a way to introduce Pune to flavors that feel personal, comforting, and unforgettable.

Meet Snehal

OUR BRAND LEAD

The first time Snehal tried the Mango Float, it instantly felt special — the kind of dessert you immediately want your friends and family to experience too.

What started as a shared moment between friends slowly evolved into something bigger: bringing a new dessert culture and experience to Pune.

Together, Luna’s blends Filipino authenticity with thoughtful storytelling, creating desserts that feel joyful, memorable, and deeply personal.

Made for Moments That Matter

From cozy evenings to celebrations, Luna’s desserts are created to bring people together — one spoon, one smile, one memory at a time.

Inspired by Filipino dessert culture and crafted fresh in Pune, every batch is made in limited quantities so each order feels personal, warm, and worth the wait.

We also create bulk dessert experiences for celebrations, gatherings, and special events.
